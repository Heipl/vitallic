This is a statistical model, not a clearance record

Estimates on this map are produced by a Bayesian model from historical conflict-event data and partial robotic survey. They are not survey results and they are not a clearance certificate. No area shown here has been declared free of mines.

Only accredited mine action operators, following the International Mine Action Standards land release process, can declare land released. Under IMAS 08.10 a conflict-event model is indirect evidence — enough to justify suspecting an area and to rank it for survey, never enough to cancel it.

  • Never enter, cross or work in any area on the basis of this map.
  • A low estimate means the model has little evidence of mines here. It does not mean there are none.
  • If you find or suspect explosive ordnance, do not approach it — mark the location from a distance and report it to the national mine action authority or emergency services.

Known limits, stated plainly: the prior ends in 2024; only 23% of Ukraine's conflict events are located to an exact point; mines are laid where fighting was prevented, which conflict data cannot see; and Mine Action Review's own 2025 verdict on Ukraine is “massive but no reliable estimate”, so the absolute counts here rest on an assumption you can change in the sidebar.
